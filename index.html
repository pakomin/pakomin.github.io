Beginnings

They say the world began with a big bang - an infinitesimally small point exploding with all the matter and energy of the entire universe combined. They say that first came the primordial soup of neutrons, positrons, and atoms. This is what the physicist would have you believe. This is wrong. First came the physicist's mother, who on bringing him into clife gave him the love and encouragement with which he looks up at the night sky. Then came his father, who, worried about the child spending too much time admiring the night sky, asks him to justify this use of time. And thus, the question "why" comes into his life, and with it all the rest of what is physics.
